# AR-98302 — DsTable sticky columns

## Goal

Let `DsTable` keep chosen columns in view during horizontal scroll. Utility columns are always sticky: drag handle, expander and selection on the left, row actions on the right. Built on a single scroll container with native `position: sticky`.

## Acceptance criteria

- With `stickyColumns={{ left: ['firstName'] }}`, the First Name header and body cells stay at the left edge (after the utility columns) while the table scrolls horizontally.
- With `stickyColumns={{ right: ['status'] }}`, Status stays at the right edge, just before the row actions column.
- Within each side, columns appear in the order of the `stickyColumns` arrays.
- The reorder, expander and select columns (when present) are always sticky left, before any consumer left columns. Listing their ids in `stickyColumns` has no effect.
- The row actions column (when present) is always sticky right, after any consumer right columns. Listing its id in `stickyColumns` has no effect.
- `onStickyColumnsChange` receives consumer ids only, never utility column ids.
- Uncontrolled: with no `stickyColumns` prop, the table keeps its own state. Controlled: the `stickyColumns` prop wins. This mirrors `columnVisibility` / `onColumnVisibilityChange`.
- The last left-sticky cell shows the edge divider (inset border plus a 2px shadow) only while content is scrolled under it (`scrollLeft > 0`).
- The first right-sticky cell shows the edge divider only while more content remains to the right. The divider disappears when scrolled fully right.
- Toggling the divider does not shift layout.
- Listing a group id, or any leaf of a group, pins every leaf of that top-level group. The group header renders once and is sticky.
- A sticky fill column (no `size`) renders at a fixed width (its `getSize()`, 150px by default), so offsets stay correct.
- With `resizableColumns`, resizing a sticky column moves the offsets of the sticky columns after it live during the drag.
- A sticky column hidden via `columnVisibility` is not rendered, and its id stays in `stickyColumns`. Showing it again makes it sticky again.
- Sticky cells pick up the row background (default, hover, selected, active, dragging), so scrolled content never shows through.
- The header stays at the top during vertical scroll, as it does today. `stickyHeader` keeps its current behavior and API.
- The vertical scrollbar sits at the visible right edge of the table, including when the table overflows horizontally.
- Virtualized tables:
  - Sticky columns work as in non-virtualized tables.
  - Row positions stay correct while scrolling.
  - The last row is reachable.
  - `onScroll` and `infiniteScroll.onLoadMore` fire from the single scroll container.
- Expanded rows (`renderExpandedRow`, virtualized and not):
  - The panel content stays within the visible area while the table scrolls horizontally.
  - The panel background spans the full row.
  - A nested `DsTable` inside the panel fits the visible width, scrolls on its own, and its own sticky columns work.
- Drag-to-reorder rows still works with sticky utility and consumer columns.
- Empty state, loading skeleton, column filters, editing and the row actions menus behave as before.

## Decisions

- **Technique:** single scroll container (`.dataTableContainer` / `.virtualizedContainer` scroll both axes).
  - `thead` uses `position: sticky; top: 0`.
  - Sticky cells use `position: sticky` with an offset in `--ds-table-sticky-offset`.
  - The JS `translateX` technique was rejected after the spike because of scroll-linked lag, RTL complexity, and the scrollbar staying off-screen.
- **API:**
  - `stickyColumns?: DsTableStickyColumns` (`{ left?: string[]; right?: string[] }`) and `onStickyColumnsChange?(next)`.
  - Mapped to TanStack `state.columnPinning`.
  - No new methods on the imperative `DsTableApi`.
- **Scope:** pinning in `DsTable` only. No column-manager component; a story shows one built with `DsPinToggle`.
- **Utility columns:** always sticky, cannot be unpinned.
- **Edge divider:** shown only while content is under it, on both sides. Visual spec from Figma: `drop-shadow(-2px 0 2px rgba(0,0,0,.15))` plus a border.
- **Column groups:** pinning any leaf, or the group id, pins the whole top-level group.
- **Resize:** sticky columns stay resizable. Offsets use `calc()` over the `--col-*-size` CSS vars while `resizeSizingReady`, and pixel sums otherwise.
- **Hidden columns:** keep their pin and are not auto-unpinned.
- **Virtualized and infinite scroll:** both supported.
  - The virtualizer scrolls the outer container, with `scrollMargin` set to the header height.
  - `tbody` height equals the virtualizer's total size.
- **Expanded rows:** the content wrapper is `position: sticky; left: var(--standard)`, with width = visible width minus padding. Measure the visible width with `clientWidth`, which excludes the vertical scrollbar.
- **Sticky fill columns:** fixed at `getSize()`.
- **`stickyHeader`:** unchanged; the header is always sticky.
- **Sticky columns wider than the viewport:** no handling; documented.
- **Virtualized table in a parent with no height limit:** no guard; document that a bounded height is required, as today.
- **Focus under sticky columns** (`scroll-padding`): out of scope.
- **Scrollbar-spacer machinery:** becomes redundant under a single scroll container.

## Out of scope

- Column-manager component (search, visibility, pin toggles).
- Keeping focused cells clear of sticky columns.
- Any handling when sticky columns exceed the viewport width.
- A guard or warning for virtualized tables without a bounded height.
- Changing `stickyHeader` semantics or deprecating it.
